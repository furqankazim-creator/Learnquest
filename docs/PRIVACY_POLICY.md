# Privacy Policy — Kids Learning App

_Last updated: 2026-11-16_

Kids Learning App ("the app") is an educational game for children in school Years 1–6 (ages 5–11). We built it so that **no personal information leaves the device.**

## What we collect
**Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.** The app has no accounts, no login, no advertising, no analytics, no chat and no tracking.

## What is stored on the device
To remember progress between visits, the app saves the following **only on the device itself**:
- a first name (optional — the child can play as "Guest Explorer"),
- the chosen buddy and school year,
- stars, coins, stickers, questions answered and a play-day streak,
- sound and colour-theme settings,
- a 4-digit grown-ups' PIN chosen by the parent.

This information never leaves the device. We ask for a first name only — never an email address, birthday, photo, location or contact details.

## Permissions
The app does **not** request access to the microphone, camera, contacts, location or photos.

## Voice
Questions are read aloud using the text-to-speech voice built into the device. The app does not record audio.

## Third parties
The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics or social-media code, and shows no links that leave the app.

## Deleting data
A grown-up can erase progress at any time from **Parent tab → Reset stars, coins and progress**, or by uninstalling the app, which removes everything the app stored.

## Children
The app is designed for children and complies with the principle of data minimisation: it collects no personal data from children. Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to share, sell or disclose.

## Changes
If this policy changes we will update the date above and the text inside the app.
