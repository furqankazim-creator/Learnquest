# Privacy Policy — Kids Learning App

_Last updated: 2026-11-21_

Kids Learning App ("the app") is an educational game for children in school Years 1–6 (ages 5–11). We built it so that **personal information is not collected by us and does not leave the device except when you choose to open an external link.**

## What we collect
**We do not collect or receive your data.** The app has no accounts, no login, no advertising, no analytics, no chat and no tracking.

## What is stored on the device
To remember progress between visits, the app saves the following **only on the device itself**:
- a first name (optional — the child can play as "Guest Explorer"),
- the chosen buddy and school year,
- stars, coins, stickers, questions answered and a play-day streak,
- sound and colour-theme settings,
- a 4-digit grown-ups' PIN chosen by the parent,
- custom questions created by a grown-up (including optional pictures you choose from the photo library).

This information stays on the device. We ask for a first name only — never an email address, birthday, precise location or contact details.

## Permissions
- **Photo library (optional):** used only when a grown-up chooses a picture for a custom question. Selected images are copied into the app's on-device storage and are never uploaded or shared by the app.
- **Microphone:** not used and audio is not recorded.
- **Camera, contacts, precise location:** not requested.

## Voice
Questions and feedback can be read aloud using the text-to-speech voice built into the device. The app does not record audio.

## External links
The app includes optional publisher/support links (for example website and email) in the Discover/About & support areas. Opening these links leaves the app and is handled by your device/browser or email app; those services have their own privacy practices.

## Third parties
The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics or social-media SDKs.

## Deleting data
A grown-up can erase progress at any time from **Parent tab → Reset stars, coins and progress**, or by uninstalling the app, which removes the data the app stored on the device.

## Children
The app is designed for children and follows data minimisation: it does not collect personal data for our servers. Because nothing is collected by us, there is nothing for us to share, sell or disclose.

## Changes
If this policy changes we will update the date above and the in-app policy details.
