## Full description
Learn English, Maths and Science through four friendly games — Balloon Pop, Rescue Quiz, Match It and Quick Quiz — plus a Daily Mission mixing all three subjects.

• Made for Years 1 to 6 (ages 5–11). Questions get harder every year: Year 1 starts with sounds and counting, Year 6 reaches fractions, ratio, algebra, evolution and the circulatory system.
• Every question is read aloud, so early readers can play too.
• Hundreds of questions, with fresh Maths numbers every game and a short "why" after answers.
• Stars, coins and stickers are only ever earned — never lost. No timers, no fail screens, gentle encouragement on every wrong answer.
• Pick a buddy, build a daily streak and collect all 12 stickers.
• Grown-ups' Area (PIN-protected) shows progress by subject and gives a "practise tonight" tip.
• Works offline. Light and dark themes.

PRIVACY: No ads, no chat, no accounts, no tracking. Only an optional first name is kept, and it stays on the device.
